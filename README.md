# Alexandre

**Anesthesiologist & Intensivist | PhD Candidate in Neuroscience**

I am a physician trained in anesthesiology and critical care, working in a surgical intensive care unit. My clinical and research focus is **continuous EEG monitoring in patients with acute brain injury**.

## 🔬 Doctoral research
Early detection of **delayed cerebral ischemia (DCI) after aneurysmal subarachnoid hemorrhage**, combining continuous EEG and ECG-derived heart rate variability into a bedside predictive tool.

University of Rennes, Brittany, France :
- Empenn IRISA (U1228 INSERM-INRIA)
- CinetyKs LTSI (UMR INSERM 1099)


## 🧠 Research interests
- Status epilepticus
- Cerebral ischemia
- Quantitative EEG
- Functional connectivity
- Heart rate variability
- Heart–brain interactions
- Predictive modeling
